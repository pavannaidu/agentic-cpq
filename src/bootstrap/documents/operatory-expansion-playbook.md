# Operatory Expansion Playbook

Two-chair growth conversations should connect equipment, sterilization, starter consumables, and onboarding in one story. Sellers should explain that an operatory bundle is not just hardware; it is the fastest route to a productive room with fewer post-sale follow-ups.

Recommended talk track:

- Anchor on the practice goal: open the next operatory quickly without creating workflow gaps.
- Bundle sterilization with equipment to avoid opening-day compliance risk.
- Keep starter consumables in the quote so the room is usable immediately.
- Include software or onboarding when the practice is adding digital workflow steps at the same time.
