# Financing and Approval Guide

Use financing positioning when capital purchases start to crowd out the customer's budget or when the customer wants to preserve cash for staffing and marketing. Agentic CPQ prepares the customer-ready document; any financing discussion or application remains in the organization's approved downstream process.

Do not provide APRs, monthly payments, lease terms, lender options, amortization schedules, or financing plan comparisons unless those terms exist in governed guidance. Do not claim that the copilot originates loans, collects payment, or captures signatures. Keep guidance seller-facing and generate the approved quote PDF for customer review.

For the demo, sellers should keep three messages consistent:

1. Financing positioning is most relevant to equipment and imaging packages that materially change cash flow.
2. Approval-sensitive discounts still need to be confirmed in CPQ before the quote is sent.
3. Services and onboarding should not be the first items removed from the draft quote because they reduce implementation risk and speed time to value.
